LocalAI runs models of many kinds. Each page here shows what a kind of model does, how to call it and which backends serve it. The API features are on by default for new users.

| Group | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Text and retrieval]({{% relref "/models/capabilities/text" %}}) | Text generation, constrained grammars, thinking models, embeddings, reranking, moderation, time-series forecasting and benchmarks. |
| [Audio and speech]({{% relref "/models/capabilities/audio" %}}) | Speech to text, text to speech, sound classification, speaker diarization, audio transforms, voice activity and voice recognition. |
| [Images, video and 3D]({{% relref "/models/capabilities/vision" %}}) | Image understanding, object detection, face recognition, image and video generation, 3D generation and animation, and motion capture. |

Some of these have an add-on page: [Studio]({{% relref "/addons/catalogue/models-and-media/studio" %}}), [Voice]({{% relref "/addons/catalogue/models-and-media/voice" %}}), [Face]({{% relref "/addons/catalogue/models-and-media/face" %}}) and [Talk]({{% relref "/addons/catalogue/models-and-media/talk" %}}). See the [add-on catalogue]({{% relref "/addons/catalogue" %}}).
