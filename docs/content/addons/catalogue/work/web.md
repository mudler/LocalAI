Web replaces the built-in `web_search` and `web_fetch` tools. The built-ins need no key. Install this add-on to search with Brave, Tavily or Google Programmable Search, and to read PDFs in `web_fetch`.

The add-on has its own switch in the **Tools** menu of a chat. The switch on the **Web** row changes only the built-in tools.

## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it adds | Search providers that need a key (Brave Search, Tavily and Google Programmable Search), tried in the order an administrator sets, and the text of PDF files in `web_fetch`. |
| Needs | Nothing to start. For a search provider with a key, an administrator saves the key on the **Settings** tab of the add-on. |
