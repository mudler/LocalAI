Connect a Telegram bot to one of your saved agents. Anyone who messages the bot talks to that agent, and the answer comes back in Telegram.

## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it adds | A Telegram bot for one saved agent. Each sender gets their own conversation. It shows in the agent owner's chats. |
| Needs | The Saved agents (`agents`) and Chat connectors (`connectors`) permissions. Both are off by default for new users on a server with accounts. A new bot answers nobody until you add its allowed senders. |
