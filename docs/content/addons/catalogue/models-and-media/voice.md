Voice adds a page where you recognise who is speaking, keep the voices that text to speech can use, and speak text with them.

## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it adds | One page, `/app/addons/voice/voice`, with four tabs: **Recognition**, **Library**, **Text to speech** and **From a recording**. The Library tab is for administrators. |
| Needs | For the Recognition tab, the Voice Recognition permission (`voice_recognition`), which is on by default. |
| Guide | [Voice Recognition]({{% relref "/models/capabilities/audio/voice-recognition#the-webui-page" %}}) |
