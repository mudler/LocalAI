Face adds a page where you identify a face in a photo, check whether two photos show the same person, and manage the faces you enrolled.

## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it adds | One page, `/app/addons/face/recognition`, for the three tasks above. The detect and embedding tools are under **More tools**. |
| Needs | The Face Recognition permission (`face_recognition`), which is on by default. |
| Guide | [Face Recognition]({{% relref "/models/capabilities/vision/face-recognition#the-webui-page" %}}) |
