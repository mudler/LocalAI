# Using the LocalAI name, logo and mascot

The LocalAI code is MIT licensed. The LocalAI name, the logo and the mascot art are not part of that license: all rights reserved. The code and the art live in the same repository, but each keeps its own terms. This page says what you may do with the logo and the mascot without asking.

## You may

- Show the logo or the mascot in an article, a blog post, a talk, a slide, a video or a community post that is about LocalAI or that mentions it.
- Link to LocalAI with the logo, for example in a list of tools you use.
- Use the files as they are: the original colors, the original proportions, and a clear space around them.
- Make the logo smaller or larger, in one ratio only.

## You may not

- Change the logo or the mascot. No recoloring, stretching, rotating, outlines, shadows, glows or redrawing.
- Use them in a way that suggests that LocalAI made, approved or sponsors your product, service or event.
- Put them on products, merchandise or app icons without written permission.
- Use them in a name, a domain, a package name or the logo of another project.
- Use them in anything misleading, hateful, illegal or unrelated to LocalAI.
- Train or fine-tune a model to copy the mascot, or sell the art.

## The code

Nothing on this page limits what the MIT license allows for the code. You can fork, modify and redistribute the code under that license. If you ship a fork under a different name, use your own name and your own logo.

## Ask first

For anything outside this page, such as merchandise, a partner logo wall, a press kit, or a product that bundles LocalAI, open an issue or write to the maintainers and wait for a yes.
